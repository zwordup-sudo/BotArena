## Phase D: APTEN Operator (Shooter)
**Role Affinity:** Shooter
**Playstyle:** Area denial, routing disruption, compounding marks.

**Core - Watchdog Node:**
- **Q Ability:** Spawns a discrete grid presence that monitors the area. Enemies remaining inside continually suffer a minor slow and refresh their Compromised status.

**Chassis - Trace Cache:**
- **Passive:** Records attack events. Landing 3 distinct damage events (from abilities or normal attacks) within 3.5 seconds automatically applies Compromised to the target.

**Arms - Route Hijack:**
- **Primary (Packet Repeater):** Fires moderately fast projectiles. If they hit a Compromised target, they extend the duration of the mark up to a cap (instead of refreshing it).
- **E Ability (Route Hijack):** Fires an explosive packet. 
  - **Normal:** Damages and applies Compromised.
  - **Exploit:** If the target is Compromised, consumes the mark to violently explode, dealing damage and leaving behind an *Interference Field* at the target's location that slows enemies.

**Legs - Proxy Step:**
- **R Ability:** Executes a discrete relocation (blink) in the inputted direction. Leaves behind a Proxy Node at the start location that pulses after a short delay, slowing and applying Compromised to caught enemies.
