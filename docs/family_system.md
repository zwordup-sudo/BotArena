# Family System 0.1 Foundation

The Family System introduces a secondary, independent identity dimension to BotArena builds. While the **Role Profile** (Tank, Assassin, Shooter) dictates combat function and specialization, the **Family Profile** dictates flavor, mechanics, and compatibility.

## Final Family Names

The four final families replacing the temporary codenames are:

1. **VENTRA**
   *Identity*: Versatility, compatibility, direct power.
2. **APTEN**
   *Identity*: Control, modification, hacking, rule manipulation.
3. **PRISMA**
   *Identity*: Precision, efficiency, synchronization.
4. **MOSAIC**
   *Identity*: Adaptation, modularity, reconfiguration. (Note: "Morph" is reserved as mechanical vocabulary for this family).

## Independence of Profiles

The **Role Profile** and **Family Profile** are strictly independent:
- Role Affinity (Tank/Assassin/Shooter) is completely separated from Family Affinity.
- A build can theoretically be 100% Tank while simultaneously being 100% Ventra, or a 50/50 mix of Tank/Shooter while being a 25/25/25/25 mix of the four families.

## Family Coverage

Family Coverage is calculated based on the number of physical parts carrying *any* family affinity (maximum 4 slots: Core, Chassis, Arms, Legs).
`Family Coverage = (Count of physical parts with > 0 Family Affinity) / 4`

Current Lab pieces (Standard, Light, Heavy, Tank Test, Assassin Test, Shooter Test) possess **zero** family affinity and do not contribute to Family Coverage.

## No Purity Stat Bonus

Specializing in a single family (e.g., 100% VENTRA) will **not** grant hidden raw stat bonuses (such as +10% Damage). The reward for family specialization is achieved through piece synergy, family-specific mechanics, Protocol access, and coherent gameplay styles rather than arbitrary statistical advantages.

## Protocol Intersection

The future Protocol catalogue available to a player will be determined by:
`Role Profile + Family Profile + Coverage/Compatibility`

Two builds with identical Role Profiles but different Family Profiles will have access to different specialized Protocol options. 
*Note: Family filtering is currently inactive to preserve neutral Protocols during prototype testing.*

## Development Strategy

The eventual canonical roster will feature 48 pieces:
`4 Families × 3 Roles × 4 Physical Pieces = 48 Canonical Pieces`
Development continues using a vertical-slice approach, introducing specific features progressively without disrupting the neutral combat baseline.
